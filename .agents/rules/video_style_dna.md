# Video Production Style DNA (Trained from Reference Masters)

Whenever generating images, videos, audio, or compositions in this project, enforce the following quality benchmarks:

1. **Optics & Depth of Field:**
   - Always simulate fast cinema prime lenses (50mm/85mm at `f/1.8`–`f/2.2`).
   - Crisp subject in focus with organic background bokeh. No flat CGI rendering.

2. **Lighting & Contrast:**
   - Cinematic low-key lighting with 4:1+ contrast.
   - Distinct warm amber edge/rim light (`3200K`) separating subjects from cool, dark, or celestial backgrounds (`5600K`–`6500K`).

3. **Motion Cadence:**
   - Smooth, deliberate orbital or dolly camera movement (GSAP scale `1.00` → `1.06` over 4–6s).
   - Each visual scene must hold for at least 4.0s to allow emotional immersion.

4. **Audio Layering:**
   - Voice: Deep, calm, philosophical resonance (`en-US-BrianNeural` or `hi-IN-MadhurNeural`).
   - 3-layer audio mix: Centered vocal narration + subtle environmental foley (-18 dB) + sub-bass ambient drone bed (-22 dB).
